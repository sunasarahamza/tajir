Who We Are

Wholesale Trading Built on Genuine Integrity

Tajir is a dedicated wholesale trading enterprise specializing in the sourcing, handling, and bulk supply of premium commodities.

What We Do: We act as the trusted bridge between primary producers, agricultural cooperatives, and commercial businesses. Our trading operations encompass wholesale vegetables, seasonal fruits, specialty areca nuts and almonds, essential spices, and distributed beverages.

Trading Activities: From meticulous farm-gate grading and cold-chain logistics to wholesale parcel allocation, we serve supermarkets, institutional caterers, food processors, and regional trading partners. We simplify complex supply chains through honest, prompt, and responsive commercial trading.

Our Commitment to Customers: In wholesale commerce, trust is the highest currency. We stand behind the grade, freshness, and packaging specifications agreed with each client, providing clear communication from initial WhatsApp inquiry to scheduled dispatch.

Principles That Guide Us

Our Core Values

Every transaction, quotation, and client partnership at Tajir is governed by five foundational pillars.

1. Integrity

The heartbeat of our brand. We honor our commitments, keep pricing honest, and maintain transparent trading ethics.

2. Quality

We never compromise on produce grading, moisture benchmarks, or packaging resilience during storage and transport.

3. Transparency

Direct, straightforward communication regarding market availability, seasonal variations, and fulfillment lead times.

4. Reliability

Dependable scheduling, consistent bulk lot deliveries, and an unwavering focus on supply continuity for our clients.

Your repeat business is our true metric of success. We tailor our wholesale terms to empower your commercial profitability.
